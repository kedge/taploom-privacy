English简体中文

Taploom helps you run tapping and swiping routines that you configure and start yourself. This policy explains how the app, its advertising and payment services, and this policy website handle information.

1. Routines and settings stay on your device

Routine names, coordinates, action sequences, timing, repeat rules, target-app bindings and app preferences are stored in the app’s private storage. Taploom does not provide an app account or a cloud routine service, and does not upload these routines to a developer-operated server. Android system cloud backup is disabled for the app.

If you export or import a routine, Android’s file picker lets you choose the location or document provider. The provider you choose may be a cloud provider with its own policy. Exported copies remain under your control and are not removed when you uninstall Taploom.

2. Accessibility service

The accessibility service is optional and must be enabled by you in Android settings after an in-app disclosure. It performs the configured taps, long presses and swipes when you start a routine. It uses necessary window and foreground-app information to check whether execution has left the app you selected.

Taploom does not use this service to read screen text, a complete view hierarchy, typed content or screenshots. Routine coordinates, target-app information and accessibility events are not supplied to advertising partners as targeting data. You can stop a routine, close the control panel or disable the accessibility service at any time.

3. Advertising and consent

The free version integrates the InMobi advertising SDK directly and uses InMobi CMP to manage advertising privacy choices. Ads appear in the main app; banners are removed while the automation control panel is visible, a routine is running, or the main app is in the background.

Advertising services are enabled only after an on-device confirmation that you are at least 18 and the applicable privacy conditions are met. We store an age-group answer, not your date of birth. Skipping or declining this step keeps the core tool available. Consent errors or unknown required consent states prevent ad requests.

When enabled, InMobi, its advertising demand partners and CMP services may process advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location inferred from IP, device and app information, consent signals, ad interactions and diagnostic or fraud-prevention information. These data support ad delivery, measurement, consent management, security and fraud prevention, and personalized advertising where permitted by your choices. The app does not request precise location permission or deliberately send your routines or screen contents to these services.

You can review or change advertising choices in Settings → Manage ad privacy. Depending on your region, the CMP provides consent or opt-out controls for purposes and vendors. You can also use Android’s advertising privacy controls. A verified ad-removal purchase stops banner requests and display in Taploom; it does not remove ads in other apps.

See the InMobi privacy policy and InMobi opt-out information for its data practices, partners, retention and rights channels.

4. Purchases

If you purchase ad removal, Google Play processes the payment. Taploom uses purchase records, tokens, signatures and acknowledgement status to verify and restore the entitlement, and stores the necessary verification data locally. The app does not receive your payment card number. Google controls the purchase records it maintains. See Google’s privacy policy.

5. Support and sharing

If you email support, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and resolve the issue. Please avoid sending passwords, payment details, screen contents or routines containing sensitive information. We do not sell your routines or use support messages for advertising.

Information may be processed by the services described above, by service providers needed to handle your support request, or where required by applicable law. Advertising services may share relevant ad-request information with their demand partners as described in their policies and the CMP vendor disclosures. We do not operate a separate routine-sharing backend.

6. Retention, deletion and your choices

Routines and preferences remain until you delete them, clear app storage or uninstall the app. Private purchase and consent caches can also be removed by clearing app storage. You must delete exported files separately. Clearing local storage does not cancel a purchase or delete data already held by Google Play or advertising partners.

Support correspondence is kept as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records and meet applicable obligations; you can ask us to delete it. Third-party retention is governed by their policies and applicable requirements. We do not promise a retention period on their behalf.

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal information, or to withdraw consent or opt out. Contact us for information we control. For advertising or Play records controlled by those providers, use their privacy channels; we can help identify the relevant provider.

7. Security and international processing

We use Android app-private storage and limit permissions to the functions described here. No security method provides an absolute guarantee. The developer is based in China, and service providers may process information outside your country. Applicable safeguards and rights are described in their policies and the laws that apply.

8. Children

Taploom is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children. The app does not enable advertising services for users who report being under 18 or skip the age question. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us so we can review and handle it.

9. This policy website and updates

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. It contains no analytics scripts, tracking pixels or advertising. GitHub may process technical request information to host and secure the website under the GitHub privacy statement.

We will update this page when relevant practices change and revise the date above. Material changes that require notice or renewed consent will be communicated in the app as appropriate.

Taploom（轻点工作台）用于执行由你配置并主动启动的点击与滑动任务。本政策说明应用、广告和支付服务，以及本政策网页如何处理信息。

1. 本地任务与设置

任务名称、坐标、动作顺序、间隔、循环规则、目标应用绑定和偏好设置保存在应用的设备私有存储中。Taploom 不提供应用账号或任务云服务，不将这些任务上传至开发者运营的服务器；应用已关闭 Android 系统云备份。

导入或导出通过 Android 文件选择器进行，你可以自行选择位置或文档服务商。所选服务可能属于云服务，并适用其自身政策。导出副本由你管理，卸载 Taploom 不会删除这些副本。

2. 无障碍服务

无障碍服务是可选功能。阅读应用内说明后，需要你自行在 Android 设置中开启。服务在你启动任务后执行已配置的点击、长按和滑动，并使用必要的窗口及前台应用信息，检查是否已离开所绑定的目标应用。

Taploom 不通过该服务读取屏幕文字、完整控件树、输入内容或截图。任务坐标、目标应用信息和无障碍事件不会作为广告定向数据提供给广告合作方。你可随时停止任务、关闭控制条或关闭系统无障碍服务。

3. 广告与隐私选择

免费版本直接接入 InMobi 广告 SDK，并使用 InMobi CMP 管理广告隐私选择。广告位于主应用内；自动化控制条可见、任务运行或主应用进入后台时，会移除横幅广告。

广告服务仅在设备上确认你已满 18 岁，并满足适用隐私条件后启用。应用保存年龄组回答，不记录出生日期。跳过或拒绝不影响核心工具使用；同意服务出错或必要状态未知时，不发起广告请求。

启用后，InMobi、其广告需求合作方和 CMP 服务可能处理广告标识、IP 地址、通过 IP 推断的大致位置、设备及应用信息、同意信号、广告交互、诊断和反欺诈信息，用于广告投放、效果衡量、同意管理、安全及反欺诈，并在你的选择允许时用于个性化广告。本应用不申请精确定位权限，也不会主动将任务或屏幕内容发送给这些服务。

你可在设置 → 管理广告隐私中查看或更改选择。CMP 会按适用地区提供用途、厂商同意或退出选项，也可使用 Android 广告隐私设置。已验证的去广告购买会停止 Taploom 内的横幅请求和展示，不会移除其他应用中的广告。

关于合作方、保留期限和隐私权利渠道，请查看 InMobi 隐私政策及其退出说明。

4. 购买

去广告购买由 Google Play 处理支付。应用使用购买记录、令牌、签名和确认状态验证及恢复权益，并在本地保存必要的验证数据。应用不会取得你的支付卡号。Google 自行管理其平台购买记录，详见 Google 隐私政策。

通过电子邮件联系客服时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址和主动提供的内容，用于答复及解决问题。请避免发送密码、支付信息、屏幕内容或含有敏感信息的任务。我们不会出售任务，也不会将客服邮件用于广告。

本政策列出的服务、处理客服请求所需的服务商，或适用法律要求的接收方可能处理有关信息。广告服务可按其政策及 CMP 厂商披露，向需求合作方传递有关广告请求信息。我们没有独立的任务共享后台。

6. 保存、删除与选择

任务与设置保存到你删除、清除应用存储或卸载为止。清除应用存储也可移除应用私有的购买和同意缓存。导出文件需另行删除。清除本地数据不会取消购买，也不会删除 Google Play 或广告合作方已经保存的数据。

客服邮件仅在处理请求、保存相关记录及履行适用义务所需范围内保留，你可以申请删除。第三方信息的保留受其政策和适用要求约束，我们不代其承诺具体保留期限。
